About

EMI

@whatis.emi

EMI is unbeatable on the mic and behind the DJ decks. She stands for glamorous showgirl aesthetics and combines theatre with contemporary drag performance.

Symba

@symba4ever

Known in Berlin as Face & Grace and in demand internationally, Symba brings glamour to every performance with versatile, self-created looks.

Magda von Pfeffer

@magda_von_pfeffer

Magda combines classic travesty with contemporary drag aesthetics, writes her own songs and performs them live. On stage she plays a gloriously comic tipsy lush; off stage she is an activist.

Drag as Stage Art

The Berlin drag collective DUCT TAPE was founded in 2022 by EMI, Symba and Magda von Pfeffer. It combines drag with theatre, comedy, dance, music and performance art, and conceives its shows as complete works of art with their own dramaturgy, their own visual language and a clear commitment to quality.

Its productions include the self-written Christmas musical HOLY GLORY and the YouTube series HOT STUFF with eBay, alongside regular formats such as the Drag Bingo Nights and the Drag Brunch with fritz-kola. In 2024 DUCT TAPE received the REMIX Popculture Icon Award for its cultural engagement, in 2025 the collective performed with 25 artists in front of around 30,000 people on the main stage of Berlin Pride (CSD), and in 2026 an India tour with Gaysi Family and the Goethe-Institut took it to Mumbai, New Delhi and Kolkata.
