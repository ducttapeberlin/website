Cabaret, reinterpreted

Magda von Pfeffer

Magda von Pfeffer is a drag queen, singer, comedian and host from Berlin. She brings together old-school travesty, German cabaret and chanson with contemporary drag.

On stage Magda is loud, cheeky and a little chaotic. She talks fast, thinks even faster and turns every little thing into a story until the whole room is laughing. Then it suddenly goes quiet and she sings: chansons, cabaret numbers and her own songs, live. Anyone who has seen her as a hilarious tipsy lush will not forget that character in a hurry. Away from the stage she is an activist.
