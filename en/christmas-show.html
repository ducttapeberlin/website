The show

For its 2026 Christmas show, DUCT TAPE brings the Berlin drag scene together for two nights at Theater im Delphi. Only Berlin-based artists take the stage, and each night has its own line-up and programme.

The range runs from glamorous Christmas fantasy and big show moments to festive chaos: drag, comedy, live vocals, dance and performance. The show is presented by Anytime Studios.
