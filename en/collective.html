The collective

DUCT TAPE is a Berlin drag collective founded in 2022 by EMI, Symba and Magda von Pfeffer. It produces drag shows, events and cultural projects and works with drag and performance artists from Berlin, across Germany and abroad.

Story

From a first night to a fixture

It did not start with a business plan but with an idea: put on a drag show together and, alongside it, write a Christmas musical of their own. EMI, Symba and Magda von Pfeffer were all at the start of their drag careers. The first show in September 2022 at the Salöön at Holzmarkt was also the first appearance under the name DUCT TAPE, and it sold out straight away.

Since then DUCT TAPE has celebrated a big birthday show at the end of every September. A spontaneous project has become an established name for large-scale drag and performance formats in Berlin.

The name came up on a night out. Duct tape is one of the most important tools behind the scenes of a drag show: for wigs, costumes and everything that has to hold at the last minute.

Artistic approach

Drag as a total work of art

DUCT TAPE combines drag with theatre, comedy, dance, music, pop culture, camp, fashion and performance art and deliberately refuses to be pinned to a single style of drag. The collective moves between theatre drag, club drag, comedy and performance art.

Every production is conceived as its own world, with its own dramaturgy, visual language and a clear standard of quality. That includes original concepts, elaborate shoots and promo campaigns that make every show distinctive. The guiding question is always the same: what has not yet been seen on a drag stage?

In the Berlin scene DUCT TAPE fills the gap between bar shows, club performance and large theatre productions. The shows reach drag fans as well as audiences encountering the art form for the first time, and regularly give new artists a big stage.

Milestones

Special moments

2022

The first show

In September 2022 DUCT TAPE took the stage for the first time, at the Salöön at Holzmarkt. The night sold out and became the starting point for everything that followed. Since then the collective has celebrated its birthday with a big show at the end of every September.

2022

HOLY GLORY

In December 2022 the self-written drag Christmas musical HOLY GLORY was staged for the first time. It brings together music, comedy and theatre and is the collective's most ambitious original production to date.

2023

HOT STUFF with eBay

For eBay and the agency Jung von Matt, DUCT TAPE developed the YouTube talk show HOT STUFF, the first German drag talk show by a major marketplace.

2024

REMIX Popculture Icon Award

DUCT TAPE received the REMIX Popculture Icon Award for its cultural engagement.

2025

Berlin Pride main stage

On the main stage of Berlin Pride (CSD), DUCT TAPE presented a large performance production with 25 artists in front of around 30,000 people, the collective's largest audience so far.

2025

Three years of DUCT TAPE at Theater im Delphi

For its third birthday in September 2025, DUCT TAPE celebrated with a big show at Theater im Delphi. The theatre has become a regular venue, usually hosting two major shows a year.

2026

India tour

Together with Gaysi Family and the Goethe-Institut New Delhi, DUCT TAPE toured Mumbai, New Delhi and Kolkata in February 2026. The exchange continued with guests from India at the fourth anniversary show in Berlin.

The founders

Three roles, one collective

The vision of DUCT TAPE comes out of the way the three work together. Each brings her own strengths, and the roles are clearly divided.

EMI

@whatis.emi

Management, organisation, concept and hosting

As host, EMI leads the audience through the shows: quick-witted, approachable and with clear authority. Her stage persona combines sharp host energy with showgirl glamour in glitter, latex and leather. Behind the scenes she holds the organisation together and develops concepts and scripts. She also performs as a DJ.

Symba

@symba4ever

Visual aesthetics, dance, performance and social media

On stage Symba is an apparition: glamorous, sexy, confident and always striving for perfection. Inspired by American drag, every detail is in place, from the wig to the posture. Her numbers move between high energy and high drama.

Magda von Pfeffer

@magda_von_pfeffer

Comedy, vocals and on-site production

Magda brings together classic travesty, German cabaret and modern drag. Singer, host and comedian in one, fast, cheeky and unpredictable, she turns any spontaneous moment into a story. Musically she is at home in chanson and cabaret and writes her own songs.

Network

Partners and the DUCT TAPE Family

Partners, brands and institutions DUCT TAPE has worked with include: eBay, About You, Zalando, Lieferando, Lollapalooza, Tinder, No Cosmetics, Anytime Studios, Gaysi Family, Goethe-Institut New Delhi, Theater im Delphi, Holzmarkt, rausgegangen, CSD Berlin, Ritter Butzke, SCHWUZ and Cura Berlin.

Around the collective the DUCT TAPE Family has grown: a network of drag queens, performers, dancers, hosts and singers from Berlin, Germany and the international scene. Guests have included Miss Ivanka T., Rachel Intervention, Yvonne Nightstand, Hungry, Nikita Vegaz, Tessa Testicle, Jimbo the Drag Clown and Derrick Barry.
