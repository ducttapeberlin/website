How it works

Bingo with drag performances

The game runs over several bingo rounds, hosted by EMI, Symba or Magda von Pfeffer. Between the rounds there are drag performances and surprises, and every round has prizes for the winners.

Drag bingo FAQ

How much does drag bingo cost?

You play with bingo cards. Depending on the venue, taking part costs between €5 and €7 per person. Details for each date are on the linked event page.

When is drag bingo?

From autumn to spring, regularly on several evenings a month. See the dates and times above.

Where is drag bingo?

At various bars in Berlin. The venue is listed with each date above.

Can I book drag bingo for my own event?

Yes. DUCT TAPE also brings the format to company parties, private events and bars. Requests go through the booking form.

Drag bingo for your own event?

The format can also be booked for company parties, private events and bars.
