Info

In the page's words:

The Berlin-based drag collectiveDUCT TAPE, founded byEMI, Symba, and Magda von Pfeffer, is one of the most influential forces in Berlin’s drag and performance scene. With their elaborately produced shows, they blend drag with theater, comedy, dance, music, and performance art, regularly bringing queer art to the city’s major stages.

Since 2022, DUCT TAPE has been producing some of Berlin’s biggest drag shows, collaborating with numerous drag and performance artists from Berlin, all over Germany, and beyond. Their shows are known for their diverse lineups, original concepts, and a blend of glamour, humor, and a spirit of artistic experimentation.

In addition to their large-scale stage productions, the collective regularly organizesbar and club nights, including their popularDrag Bingo Nights.

The collective’s work is also gaining increasing international recognition:In 2026, DUCT TAPE was invited by the Goethe-Institut to performas partof a tour through Indiaandto bring Berlin’s drag culture into the international spotlight.

Their own productions also include the Christmas musicalHOLY GLORY, which they wrote, produced, and directed themselves and which thrilled audiences with its blend of parody, camp, and musical elements. With the YouTube seriesHOT STUFF, produced in collaboration with eBay, they expanded their artistic scope into the digital realm.

For their work, DUCT TAPE was honoredwith the Remix – Popculture Icon Award in 2024.

Today, the collective represents a new generation of drag in Berlin and aims to raise the profile of drag as an art form in its own right and make it accessible to a wider audience.
