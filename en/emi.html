Showgirl glamour with an edge

EMI

EMI is a drag queen, host and DJ from Berlin. On stage she combines sharp host energy with theatre: her stage persona is spontaneous and approachable, with clear authority. Her drag aesthetic draws on striking, glittering showgirl looks and dominant latex and leather.

Anyone who sees EMI on stage quickly notices the theatre background: she has a feel for timing, reads the room and has a quick answer for every situation. As a host she guides an evening so confidently that the audience happily follows. In her own numbers she tells stories somewhere between glamour, theatre and showgirl fantasy. And when the curtain falls, the work goes on: she develops concepts, writes scripts and holds productions together. That is why EMI does not just announce an evening, she carries it. She is booked across Germany and internationally.
