The shows: a big stage for Berlin drag

Every DUCT TAPE production is its own world with dramaturgy, visual language and a clear standard of quality. The shows close the gap between bar show, club performance and large theatre production, and regularly give new artists a big stage.

Fixed dates in the year are the Birthday Show at the end of September, when the collective celebrates its anniversary, and the Christmas shows in December.

Drag shows FAQ

Where can I get tickets?

Tickets are sold via rausgegangen. The link is next to each date above.

Where do the shows take place?

At changing venues in Berlin, for example Theater im Delphi in Weißensee. The venue is listed with each date.

Who performs?

EMI, Symba and Magda von Pfeffer of DUCT TAPE plus changing guests from the Berlin drag scene and from abroad. The line-up is on each event page.

Can I book a drag show for my own event?

Yes. DUCT TAPE also produces shows for company parties, festivals, clubs and private events. Requests go through the booking form.
