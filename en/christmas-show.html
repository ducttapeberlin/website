A Berlin Drag Queens Christmas Show

About the show

For its 2026 Christmas show, DUCT TAPE brings the Berlin drag scene together for two nights at Theater im Delphi.

On 4 and 5 December, two different Berlin all-star line-ups take the stage, featuring Berlin-based artists only.

The programme combines drag, comedy, live vocals, dance and performance, from glamorous Christmas fantasy and big show moments to festive chaos.

Each night has its own line-up and its own programme: two nights, two casts.

Show 1 Friday, 4 December

Yvonne Nightstand • Rachel Intervention • Jacky-Oh Weinhaus • Bambi Mercury • Harpy • Toylette Paypr • Lady Dina • Anna Klatsche • Bibingka

Show 2 Saturday, 5 December

Yvonne Nightstand • Sasha Glam • Anesha • Karlie Kant • Yenny Stark • Raven van Krüger • Cordova • Daniela Candela • Daisy Dick

Presented by Anytime Studios.

Venue

Theater im Delphi

Gustav-Adolf-Straße 2
13086 Berlin
