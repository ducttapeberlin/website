Face and Bombshell

Symba

Symba is a drag queen, performer and dancer from Berlin. Her showgirl glamour is inspired by international and American drag. On stage she is an apparition: glamorous, sexy, confident and always striving for perfection.

When Symba takes the stage, every eye is on her: her numbers swing between high energy and high drama and live from posture, movement and expression. Much of what you see she made herself. Her outfits and wigs are often handmade, and by showtime every detail is thought through, from make-up to posture. She takes glamour seriously, just not herself too seriously. She is booked across Germany and internationally.
