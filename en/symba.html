Symba

Face and Bombshell

Symba is a drag queen, performer and dancer. Her drag is highly polished and inspired by American drag. On stage she is an apparition: glamorous, sexy, confident and always striving for perfection.

When Symba takes the stage, every eye is on her: her numbers swing between high energy and high drama, carried by posture, movement and expression. Much of what you see she made herself. Her outfits and wigs are often handmade, and by showtime every detail is thought through, from make-up to posture.
